🧬 Enzyme Nutrition — Protocol Notes

Enzyme Nutrition (Dr. Edward Howell's framework) is a protocol emphasizing the role of food and supplemental enzymes in reducing the body's "enzyme reserve" drain. The core idea: cooking destroys natural food enzymes, forcing the pancreas and body to produce all digestive enzymes — depleting systemic enzyme potential over time.

Two enzyme types in the protocol:

  • Digestive Enzymes (with meals) — taken with food to support digestion of proteins (protease), carbohydrates (amylase), and fats (lipase). Reduces burden on the pancreas.
  • Proteolytic/Systemic Enzymes (away from food) — taken on an empty stomach so they enter circulation rather than digesting food. Supports anti-inflammatory activity, immune function, and tissue repair.
